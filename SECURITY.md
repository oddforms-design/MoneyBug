<h1>Security</h1>

<p>If you've found a security problem in MoneyBug, please report it privately rather than in a public issue: use <a href="https://github.com/oddforms-design/MoneyBug/security/advisories/new">Report a vulnerability</a>, or email <a href="mailto:support@oddforms.com?subject=Security">support@oddforms.com</a> with "Security" in the subject.</p>

<p>Please include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and your macOS and MoneyBug versions. Leave out anyone's real client data.</p>

<p>I'll reply as soon as I can and let you know when a fix ships.</p>
