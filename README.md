<h1>MoneyBug Support</h1>

<h2>Common Questions</h2>

<h3>Where is my data kept?</h3>
<p>On your Mac. MoneyBug has no account and no server, and it doesn't collect or share anything about you or your clients. Your clients, jobs, time and invoices stay in the app's own library on your Mac, and in any backups you make.</p>

<h3>How do I back up, or move to a new Mac?</h3>
<p>MoneyBug backs up your library automatically: once a day, when a new version first opens, and before you import a backup. You can see and manage these in Settings → Backups. To keep a copy somewhere of your own choosing, use File → Export Backup…. On another Mac, open MoneyBug and choose File → Import Backup…, then pick the file.</p>

<h3>Can I bring my data from another app?</h3>
<p>MoneyBug can't open other apps' libraries, but File → Import Time from CSV… reads the time reports many apps export as CSV, and adds them as a new profile. It's marked Experimental: check the preview it shows before importing.</p>

<h3>I forgot my backup password</h3>
<p>If you turned on Encrypt MoneyBug Backups, the Mac that made them remembers the key, so they open there without the password. If you also turned on Save Password in iCloud Keychain, your other Macs can open them too. Anywhere else, an encrypted backup needs its password, and without it the backup can't be opened, by you or by me. Automatic backups made while encryption was off are not encrypted.</p>

<h3>An invoice number isn't what I expected</h3>
<p>Each client has its own number format. In the client's settings, choose Customize… beside Next Invoice Number (or Edit Scheme… in an invoice's bar), and you'll see the format and the next value of each counter: Client #, Profile # and Global #. You can change a counter's next value there. Typing a number into an invoice draft uses that number without moving any counter.</p>

<h2>Report a Problem</h2>

<p>Bug reports go through this website, GitHub, which keeps everything in one place and lets you see whether someone has already hit the same thing.</p>

<p><a href="https://github.com/oddforms-design/MoneyBug/issues">Browse existing issues</a> &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="https://github.com/oddforms-design/MoneyBug/issues/new">Report a new bug</a></p>

<p>Helpful things to include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Your macOS version and MoneyBug version</li>
  <li>What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead</li>
  <li>Where in the app it happened: a job, an invoice or estimate, the template editor, the menu bar</li>
  <li>Screenshots, if relevant</li>
</ul>

<p>Issue reports are public. Invoices and job lists hold your clients' details, so please blur or crop out names, addresses, amounts and anything else private before posting a screenshot. Never attach a backup file.</p>
